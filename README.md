# Exam Preparation Platform

## Overview
Welcome to our Exam Preparation Platform designed to assist students in their journey towards academic excellence. Our platform offers:

- **Scientific Summarization**: Efficiently condense complex scientific topics into easy-to-understand summaries, enabling students to grasp crucial concepts quickly.
- **Bilingual Support**: Catering to a diverse user base, our platform supports multiple languages to ensure accessibility and comprehension for all students.
- **Email Integration**: Seamlessly stay updated with a built-in email integration feature that provides notifications, reminders, and essential information to enhance the learning experience.

## Features
- **Customizable Study Plans**: Tailor your learning journey with personalized study plans that fit your schedule and learning pace.
- **Interactive Quizzes**: Engage with content through quizzes designed to test your knowledge and reinforce learning.
- **Resource Library**: Access a rich library of resources, including articles, videos, and papers curated for comprehensive learning.
